## Create an Account

<Steps>
	<Step title="Sign Up">
		Register at [Evidence Studio](https://login.evidence.studio/sign-up) and create a new account.
		You can SSO with Google, Github or Microsoft, or sign up with an email and password.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Edit your First Report">
		You'll start on an empty report. Type markdown in the editor on the left hand side, and you'll
		get a preview of the report on the right hand side.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Publish your Report">
		When you are happy with your changes, click the "Share" button in the top right corner, and then
		choose the "Publish" button. After a few seconds, you'll see a link to your report.
	</Step>
</Steps>
